Returns

Freshly roasted coffee is a perishable product, so our return policy is designed to protect both you and the quality of the beans.

What can be returned

Unopened packages can be returned within 14 days of delivery. The package must be sealed, undamaged and in its original packaging, with the roast date label intact.

What cannot be returned

  • Opened packages, or packages where the seal has been broken, even if little coffee was used.
  • Ground coffee. Once ground, coffee loses freshness quickly, so ground products are sold only as sealed bags of whole beans.
  • Packages returned after 14 days from delivery.

If a package arrives damaged or the wrong coffee was delivered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery and we will replace it free of charge, regardless of the 14-day period.

How to request a return

  1. Email Brewly Coffee customer support with your order number and the name of the coffee you want to return.
  2. Wait for our confirmation email with the return instructions.
  3. Send the unopened package back using the return address in the confirmation email.
  4. Once we receive and check the package, we refund the product price within 5 business days to your original payment method.

Note: Return shipping costs are paid by the customer unless the package arrived damaged or was the wrong product.
