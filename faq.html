Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer here? Contact our customer support team and we will reply within one business day.

Coffee

What types of coffee does Brewly sell?

Brewly sells specialty coffee beans in two main styles: espresso coffees, such as Brewly House Blend, Budapest Night and Danube Dark, and filter coffees, such as Ethiopia Yirgacheffe, Kenya Nyeri AA and Colombia Huila Washed. We sell whole beans only.

Where is Brewly Coffee roasted?

All our coffee is roasted weekly in our roastery in Budapest, Hungary.

Do you sell ground coffee?

We sell whole beans. If you own a grinder, grind the beans just before brewing for the best flavour. We do not offer ground coffee because it loses freshness quickly.

Which coffee should I buy if I drink espresso with milk?

Brewly House Blend and Budapest Night are both designed for milk drinks. House Blend has a deeper chocolate and caramel profile, while Budapest Night is smoother and sweeter.

Returns and freshness

Can I return an opened package?

No. Only unopened packages can be returned, within 14 days of delivery. Opened packages cannot be returned because coffee is a perishable product.

What if my coffee arrives damaged?

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We will replace damaged or incorrect products free of charge.

How fresh is the coffee when it arrives?

We pack beans within a few days of roasting. Each bag shows its roast date. For the best flavour, use the beans within four weeks of that date.

Brewing

How should I prepare filter coffee?

Use 15 grams of medium-coarse ground coffee for every 250 grams of water at 93–96°C. Rinse the paper filter first, bloom the grounds with 40 grams of water for 30 seconds, then pour the rest slowly. The brew should finish in 2:30 to 3:30 minutes. Full steps are in our brewing guide.

What settings should I use for espresso?

Use 18 grams of fine ground coffee, aim for a 36-gram yield, and extract for 25–30 seconds with water at 92–94°C.
